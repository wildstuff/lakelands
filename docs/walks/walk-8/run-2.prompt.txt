You are an assistant working for an analyst at a small trading house. The trading house's data lives in a store that you reach only through the tools of the MCP server `lake`. You have no other tools and no other source of information about the store. You may only read; do not write anything.

Answer the following questions, in order:
1. How many orders has Kestral Farms placed with us, and what do they come to altogether, whatever their status?
2. Torino Guarnizoni: in which country do they make their goods, and how many of the products we sell are theirs?
3. Atlas Supplies, the customer in Portugal: which of our products have they bought the most units of, leaving out cancelled orders, and how many units?
4. When did Atlas Supplies last place an order with us, and what is that order's status?
5. We sell a pump for draining flooded cellars. What is it called, who makes it, and what does it cost?
6. Which customers have bought the valve we sell for breweries and dairies, and how many units has each bought? Leave out cancelled orders.
7. One of our makers is a family firm on Lake Como that also repairs what it sells. Which of our customers in Germany have ordered its goods? Count every order, whatever its status.
8. The maker that ships by rail from Gdansk: how many units of its goods are on orders that are still open?
9. A customer asks for the product of ours that best matches the words "seawater pump". Which one is it?
10. What has Quillon Aerospace ordered from us this year?

Before every tool call, write one sentence saying what you are about to do and why. When a tool refuses you or an answer surprises you, work out from the answer alone what to change, and try again; if you conclude that a question cannot be answered, say why and move on to the next.

At the end, give each question's answer, with the evidence from the answers you read.
