You are an assistant working for the clerk of a small shop. The shop's data lives in a store that you reach only through the tools of the MCP server `lake`. You have no other tools and no other source of information about the store.

Today is 2026-10-07.

Do the following, in order:
1. Find out what the store holds and what you are allowed to do in it.
2. Look up the customer Delta Metals and list its orders.
3. Mark Delta Metals' order O-2 as shipped.
4. Record that order O-2 was paid yesterday.
5. Cancel order O-3.
6. Record the payment for order O-1 in the ledger.
7. Report each customer's revenue for the seven days up to today.

Before every tool call, write one sentence saying what you are about to do and why. When a tool refuses you or an answer surprises you, work out from the answer alone what to change, and try again; if you conclude that a task cannot be done, say why and move on to the next.

At the end, report each task as done or not done, with the evidence from the answers.
