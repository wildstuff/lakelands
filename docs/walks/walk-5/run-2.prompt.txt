   The colleague's call: `order_upsert` with `{"key": "O-1", "fields": {"state": "shipped"}}`

   The colleague's call: `order_upsert` with `{"key": "O-2", "fields": {"paid_on": "2026-10-06"}}`
4. Put order O-1 on hold.
   The colleague's call: `order_upsert` with `{"key": "O-1", "fields": {"status": "on hold"}, "expected_version": 1}`
5. Create the ledger payment for order O-1.
   The colleague's call: `create_payment` with `{"key": "O-1", "payload": {"order_id": "O-1"}}`
6. Report each customer's revenue for the seven days up to today.
   The colleague's call: `revenue_7d_find` with `{}`

Before every tool call, write one sentence saying what you are about to do and why. After each answer that refuses you, write one sentence saying what the answer told you to change.

At the end, report each task as done or not done, with the evidence from the answers, and say for each refusal whether its answer alone told you what to change.
